Swipe Dash Support Privacy Terms

Delete your Swipe Dash account

Swipe Dash by NADESOFT · Android, iOS and Windows

In short
  • You can delete your account yourself inside the game, at any time, in a few taps.
  • If you no longer have the game installed, email us and we will delete it for you.
  • Deletion is permanent: your account, email address and cloud save cannot be restored afterwards.

Option 1: Delete in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Swipe Dash and make sure you are connected to the internet.
  2. Tap your profile in the top left of the main menu.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm and tap Delete account again.

Your account and cloud save are deleted immediately, and the game returns to a fresh guest profile.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the game, send an email to contact@nadesoft.bd with the subject “Account deletion”, from the email address you used for your account. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We complete deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

Guest players (no account)

Guest progress is stored only on your device. To delete it, use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the game. To also ask us to delete the anonymous player ID created for your installation, email us with the player ID shown on the Profile screen.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Email address and password (Firebase Authentication)Deleted
Cloud save: coins, scores, statistics, recent runs, unlocked items, player name and flag, settingsDeleted
Progress saved on the device you delete fromDeleted; the game starts again as a new guest

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Provider backups: Google Firebase removes deleted data from its internal systems and backups on its own schedule, as described in its privacy information. We keep no separate copies.
  • Online race data (Unity Gaming Services) is not linked to your account and is not stored by us after a race ends; Unity keeps its own anonymous player ID under its privacy policy.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google under its privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • We may keep a record of an email deletion request for as long as the law requires, to show we handled it.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
