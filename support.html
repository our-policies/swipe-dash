Swipe Dash Support

Help, answers and how to reach us

Contact us

Email contact@nadesoft.bd. We read every message and usually reply within 2 business days. To help us find your data, please include your device model, the game version (shown in Settings) and the player ID shown on the Profile screen.

Playing

How do I control my runner?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump over hurdles and swipe down to duck under beams. The track gets faster the longer you survive.

How do online races work?

Tap Race Online. Quick Race puts you into a match with up to 3 other players. To race friends, create a private race and share the room code; they join with the same code. Everyone gets the same track, and if you crash you are only stunned for a moment.

I can't connect to an online race.

Online races need a stable internet connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, make sure the game is updated to the latest version, then try again. Solo runs work offline.

How do I get more coins and unlock characters?

Collect coins during runs and races, and watch an optional rewarded video at the end of a run to double them. Spend coins in the shop on characters, coin shapes and trails. Coins cannot be bought with real money.

Progress and accounts

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest, and your progress is saved on your device. Create a free account with Save progress on the Profile screen to back it up in the cloud.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On your old device, create an account with Save progress. On the new device, open the Profile screen and sign in with the same email and password. Accounts work across Android, iOS and Windows.

I lost my progress.

Guest progress is stored only on the device, so it is lost if you uninstall the game or reset progress, and we cannot recover it. If you had an account, sign in again on the Profile screen to restore your cloud save.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password and enter your email. We'll send a reset link; check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

How do I change my player name or flag?

Open the Profile screen (top left of the main menu) and edit your name and flag. Please don't use your real full name or other personal information.

How do I delete my account or data?

See Delete your account and data.

Ads and privacy

How do I change my ad consent choice?

If you are in a region where consent is required, open Settings → Privacy options to review or change your choice at any time. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does the Windows version have ads?

No. Ads only appear in the free Android and iOS versions.

Is Swipe Dash suitable for children?

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. See our Privacy Policy.

Report a problem or player

To report a bug, an offensive player name or cheating, email contact@nadesoft.bd with a short description, a screenshot if you have one and, for players, their name and the time of the race.
