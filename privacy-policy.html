Swipe Dash Privacy Policy

Nadesoft · Effective 25 September 2026 · Updated for player names and flags

In short
  • You can play as a guest without giving us any personal information. If you choose to create an account, we store your email address and back up your progress in the cloud.
  • The game uses anonymous IDs so guests can play, race online and later keep their progress.
  • On Android and iOS, the free game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Swipe Dash (“the game”) is developed and published by Nadesoft (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it is shared with and what choices you have. It applies to Swipe Dash on Android, iOS and Windows.

2. Information the game collects

2.1 Game progress

Your coins, best scores, statistics (runs and races played, races won, orbs collected, distance), your latest 20 runs and races (score, orbs, distance, race position and time, date), unlocked and selected characters, coin shapes and trails, the player name and flag you choose in your profile (both optional; without them the game shows a generated name such as “Dasher4821”), and your settings (music, sound, vibration, visual effects) are saved on your device. Please do not use your real full name or other personal information as your player name.

Guests: this information stays on your device only. It is deleted when you use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the game.
Account holders: if you create an account, the same information is also stored in Google Cloud Firestore (a Firebase service) under your account, so you can restore it after reinstalling and use it on other devices (Android, iOS and Windows). Only you can read or change it.

2.2 Your account (optional)

If you choose Save progress, you create an account with your email address and a password. Firebase Authentication stores your email address and a securely hashed password; we never see your password. Your email is used to sign you in, to verify the address and to send password-reset emails you request. We do not use it for marketing. When a returning player signs in, the temporary guest account created on that device is deleted.

2.3 Anonymous sign-in (Google Firebase)

When the game starts it signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase creates a random user ID for your installation; the game shows it on the Profile screen. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account. Firebase also processes technical data needed to provide the service, such as your IP address and basic device information.

The Firebase software included in the Android version may also collect basic usage and diagnostic information (for example an app-instance ID, device model, operating-system version, app version, country and events such as first launch and session start) through Google Analytics for Firebase.

2.4 Online races (Unity Gaming Services)

If you choose Race Online, the game signs you in anonymously with Unity Authentication, which assigns a random player ID. To match players and run races, Unity Lobby and Relay process your player ID, your player name and flag, session membership and your IP address, and the game exchanges your in-race position, status, score and chosen character with the other racers in the session. Other players see your player name, flag and character in the lobby, during the race and in the results. Race data is not kept by us after the race ends.

2.5 Advertising (Google AdMob, Android and iOS)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example “x2 coins”) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. The Windows version does not show ads. Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, clicks, completed rewarded videos) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Service providers

We use these providers, who process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay) – unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, using legal safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Your choices

  • Ad consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it in the device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads); on iOS you can deny tracking when asked or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Online play is optional: if you never use Race Online, the game does not sign in to Unity Gaming Services.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account, your email address and your cloud save, inside the game and at any time. You can also email us to request deletion.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Reset progress deletes your saved progress on the device; uninstalling the game removes all local data. To ask us to delete the anonymous ID of a guest installation, email us with the player ID shown on the Profile screen.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the game does not collect your name or contact details, we may need the player ID from the Profile screen to find your data. Contact us at [your contact email]. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising except through Google AdMob as described above, where you can opt out of personalised ads.

7. Children

Swipe Dash is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and security

Guest progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Account data and cloud saves are kept until you delete your account. Anonymous guest IDs and other service data are kept by our providers for as long as needed to provide the service and according to their retention policies. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections, and cloud saves are protected by security rules so that only the account owner can access them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game or on the store listing.
